# Inventory Management Policy

## Increasing Demand

Products showing a sustained upward demand trend should follow a continuous replenishment strategy.

Safety stock should be reviewed every two weeks.

Increase reorder quantities gradually to avoid stockouts.

---

## Stable Demand

Products with low demand volatility require minimal safety stock.

Maintain regular procurement schedules.

---

## Seasonal Products

Increase inventory before expected seasonal peaks.

Monitor promotional calendars.

---

## Declining Demand

Reduce procurement quantities.

Avoid excessive inventory accumulation.

Review slow-moving stock monthly.
