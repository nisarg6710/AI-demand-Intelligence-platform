# Retail Demand Trends Report

## Executive Summary

Historical retail data shows that demand generally increases during major shopping events and seasonal campaigns.

---

## Observed Demand Patterns

- Holiday seasons produce significant sales growth.
- Promotional campaigns increase short-term demand.
- Stable products exhibit low demand volatility.
- High-growth products often maintain sustained upward trends.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Monitor seasonal demand carefully.
- Maintain sufficient inventory during promotional periods.
- Use continuous replenishment for products with stable growth.
- Reduce inventory for products with declining demand.
